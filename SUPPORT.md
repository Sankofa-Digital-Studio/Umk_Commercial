# Support and safety resources

## Immediate danger

This repository is not an emergency-response channel. Maintainers cannot monitor issues or discussions continuously and cannot dispatch police or medical assistance.

In South Africa:

- **SAPS emergency:** 10111
- **Mobile emergency:** 112
- **Gender-Based Violence Command Centre:** 0800 428 428
- **GBV Command Centre “please call me”:** `*120*7867#`

Official references: [SAPS 10111 service](https://www.saps.gov.za/services/cc_10111.php), [Department of Social Development GBV Command Centre](https://www.dsd.gov.za/index.php/21-latest-news/567-the-gender-based-violence-command-centre-is-now-live), and [South African Government Thuthuzela Care Centres](https://www.gov.za/TCC).

## Product support

- Reproducible software defect: [open a bug report](https://github.com/Sankofa-Digital-Studio/Umk_Commercial/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
- Product proposal: [open a feature request](https://github.com/Sankofa-Digital-Studio/Umk_Commercial/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
- Security or privacy vulnerability: [report privately](https://github.com/Sankofa-Digital-Studio/Umk_Commercial/security/advisories/new)
- Delivery status: review the [project board](https://github.com/orgs/Sankofa-Digital-Studio/projects/7)

Before sharing diagnostics, remove names, phone numbers, email addresses, account identifiers, coordinates, incident narratives, evidence, tokens, and screenshots containing personal information.

## Legal and medical boundaries

Repository maintainers cannot provide legal or medical advice. Generated documents are preparation aids and must be checked against current official requirements. For current South African domestic-violence forms, consult the [Department of Justice and Constitutional Development](https://www.justice.gov.za/forms/form_dva.htm).
